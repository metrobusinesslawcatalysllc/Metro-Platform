Isolation and execution boundaries

Every Admin Owner is routed to a Worker with its own D1 binding. Banking, brokerage, and binary requests are separate tenant APIs. This control-plane dashboard does not display account balances or move funds. Provisioned risk and fee values are metadata only; they do not enable or execute trades.

Financial and trading services are not provided by this control-plane interface.
