Isolation and execution boundaries

          
Every Admin Owner is routed to a Worker with its own D1 binding. Banking, brokerage, and binary requests are separate tenant APIs. This control-plane dashboard does not display account balances or move funds. Changes made here do not execute trades or provision infrastructure.
